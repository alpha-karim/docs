AlphaRelay turns a field mission into a reviewable record: events, footage, after-action report, custody evidence, and export.

## Five-step workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up devices">
    Install the pilot app, activate the device license, and sign in to the web app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a mission">
    Tap **Choose Mission Mode**, choose a scenario template, and tap **Start Mission**. Remote live starts automatically when the pilot device has validated internet. Android drone controllers use **Controller Screen Capture** by default; detected DJI SDK-controller setups can unlock **Pilot mode** after a supported-aircraft and live-connection check. With Avata 2 and Goggles 3 connected to an Android device, Goggles Mode starts the mission automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch and log events">
    Use Mission Overwatch for remote live, AI live watch when configured, and command-side event logging by quick button, keyboard, or browser dictation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the mission">
    Upload or sync footage, then review with **Play with Events** and **AI Review** when configured. Analyze the media findings, then use **Ask AI** for questions grounded in the mission imagery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalize the record">
    Generate the report, seal evidence, get supervisor approval, and export when ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>
